# The Tenant's Lease Termination Was Effective on April 30

Northgate Dental Group's notice terminating its office lease was effective on April 30. The landlord, Calder Realty, received Northgate's termination notice on March 31, and everything follows from that date.

## Facts

Section 18 of the lease permits the tenant to terminate on written notice, with termination effective 30 days after the landlord receives the notice. (Lease, Ex. A, § 18.)

Northgate sent a written termination notice by certified mail. Calder received that notice on March 31. (Certified Mail Receipt, Ex. B.)

## Argument

Calder does not and cannot dispute that its receipt of the notice occurred on March 31; the certified mail receipt bears its agent's signature with that date. As of March 31, then, Calder had Northgate's written notice in hand.

Under Section 18, the 30-day period began when Calder received the notice. Because Calder got the notice on March 31, the period ran through April 30, and the termination became effective that day.

## Conclusion

The termination was effective on April 30 because Calder Realty received Northgate's written notice on March 31. Calder's claim for rent after April 30 should be dismissed.
