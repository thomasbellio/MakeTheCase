# How to File a Small Claims Case in Westbrook County

This guide explains the steps for filing a small claims case in the Westbrook County Civil Court. Small claims court handles disputes involving $10,000 or less.

## Before you file

1. Make sure your claim is within the $10,000 limit. If it is larger, you may choose to reduce it to $10,000, but you cannot later sue for the rest.
2. Ask the other party for payment in writing. Keep a copy of your letter and any reply.
3. Find the other party's correct legal name and address. For a business, you can search the Westbrook Secretary of State's online business registry.

## Filing your claim

1. Complete Form SC-100, the Plaintiff's Claim. The form is available at the clerk's office and on the court's website.
2. File the completed form with the Civil Clerk at 400 Harbor Street, Room 112, or file it online through the court's e-filing portal.
3. Pay the filing fee. Fees range from $30 to $75 depending on the amount of your claim. If you cannot afford the fee, ask the clerk for a fee waiver form.

## After you file

1. The clerk will assign a hearing date, usually 30 to 70 days after filing.
2. You must arrange for the other party to be served with a copy of your claim at least 15 days before the hearing. You may not serve the papers yourself.
3. Bring two copies of all your documents, photos, and receipts to the hearing, along with any witnesses.

For more help, visit the Small Claims Advisor in Room 118, open weekdays from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
