# The Appeal Was Timely Filed

The Board of Licensing Appeals should reject the Department's motion to dismiss, because Corwin Bakery's appeal was timely. Corwin filed its notice of appeal on June 30. (R. at 15.) That filing was timely on each of three independent grounds, any one of which is sufficient.

**First, the appeal was filed within 30 days of mailing.** Rule 4(a) of the Board's Rules of Procedure provides that an appeal is timely if filed within 30 days after the Department mails its decision. The Department mailed its decision on June 3. (R. at 12.) June 30 is 27 days after June 3, so the appeal was filed within the Rule 4(a) period.

**Second, even measured from the decision date, the deadline was extended by a holiday.** The Department argues the period runs from the date printed on the decision, May 30. Even on that view, the thirtieth day was June 29, which was Founders' Day, a legal holiday under Westbrook law. (Westbrook Gov't Code § 6.01.) Rule 4(c) provides that when the last day of a filing period falls on a legal holiday, the period extends to the next business day. The next business day was June 30, the day Corwin filed.

**Third, the Board Chair extended the deadline.** Rule 6 authorizes the Board Chair to extend any filing deadline for good cause. On June 20, the Chair granted Corwin an extension to July 15. (R. at 14.) Corwin filed on June 30, before the extended deadline.

Each of these grounds independently establishes that the appeal was timely. The motion to dismiss should be denied.
