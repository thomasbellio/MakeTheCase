# The Board Should Grant the Hendersons' Setback Variance

Tomas and Ellen Henderson ask the Zoning Board of Appeals for a variance from the 25-foot rear setback to build a single-story addition at 18 Ridgeway Lane. The Board should grant it.

Section 9.4 of the Westbrook Zoning Ordinance authorizes a variance where strict application of a setback causes a practical difficulty that is unique to the property and was not created by the owner. (Zoning Ord. § 9.4.)

Strict application of the setback causes a practical difficulty. The Hendersons' lot slopes at 30 percent across its rear half. (Survey, Ex. 1.) The project architect has determined that, because of that slope, no addition of usable size can be built within the setback without extensive retaining walls that would cost more than the addition itself. (Letter of M. Osei, AIA, Ex. 2.)

That difficulty is unique to the property. No other lot on Ridgeway Lane has a comparable slope.

The Hendersons did not create the condition. The slope is shown on the 1962 subdivision plat, decades before the Hendersons purchased the lot in 2015. (Plat, Ex. 3; Deed, Ex. 4.)

Each requirement of Section 9.4 is met, and the variance should be granted.
