# Halvorsen Construction Is Entitled to Final Payment

Halvorsen Construction completed the renovation of the Pinecrest Library and is entitled to final payment under its contract with the County.

Section 14 of the construction contract provides that final payment is due once the work has been certified by the City Building Inspector. (Contract, Ex. A, § 14.)

Section 3.1 of the Westbrook Building Code provides that any work certified by the City Building Inspector is deemed compliant with the Building Code. (Westbrook Bldg. Code § 3.1.)

Halvorsen's work is fully compliant with the Building Code. An independent structural engineer inspected the completed renovation and found no code deficiencies of any kind. (Report of R. Amari, P.E., Ex. B.)

Because certified work is code-compliant, and Halvorsen's work is code-compliant, Halvorsen's work has the certification that Section 14 requires. Final payment is therefore due.
