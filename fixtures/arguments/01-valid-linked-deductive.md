# Argument: Harlow Properties Failed to Comply with Section 14.2

Ms. Adaeze Okafor rented Unit 4B at 210 Linden Street under a written one-year lease. That lease is a residential tenancy governed by the Westbrook Residential Tenancy Act (the "Act"). (Lease, Ex. 1, at 1.) Harlow Properties, LLC is the landlord under the lease. (_Id._)

Section 14.2 of the Act requires a landlord, within 21 days after a tenant vacates the premises, either to return the tenant's security deposit in full or to deliver an itemized statement of deductions. Westbrook Residential Tenancy Act § 14.2.

Ms. Okafor vacated Unit 4B and returned her keys on March 1. (Okafor Decl. ¶ 3; Key Receipt, Ex. 2.) As of April 15, Harlow Properties had neither returned any part of her deposit nor delivered an itemized statement of deductions. (Okafor Decl. ¶¶ 5–6; Harlow Resp. to Interrog. No. 4.) April 15 is 45 days after March 1, well beyond the 21-day period the Act allows.

Because Ms. Okafor's tenancy is governed by the Act, because Harlow Properties is her landlord, and because Harlow Properties did neither of the two things Section 14.2 requires within 21 days of her vacating the unit, Harlow Properties failed to comply with Section 14.2 of the Act.
