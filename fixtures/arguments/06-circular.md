# Coastline Freight Complied with the Brake Inspection Requirement

The Department of Transportation cited Coastline Freight for failing to inspect the brakes on Truck 14 at the required intervals. The citation should be vacated, because Coastline complied with the requirement.

Westbrook Motor Carrier Regulation 7.3 requires a carrier to inspect the brakes on each commercial vehicle at least once every 90 days. (Reg. 7.3.) Coastline's maintenance log for Truck 14 records brake inspections on February 2, April 28, and July 19, each within 90 days of the last. (Maintenance Log, Ex. 2.)

The maintenance log is a reliable record. Its entries match, line for line, the monthly compliance summaries Coastline submitted to its insurer. (Compliance Summaries, Ex. 3.) Those summaries are accurate, because they are compiled directly from the maintenance log, which, as shown, is a reliable record of Coastline's inspections.

Because the log is reliable, the inspections it records actually took place. Truck 14's brakes were therefore inspected at least once every 90 days, Coastline complied with Regulation 7.3, and the citation should be vacated.
