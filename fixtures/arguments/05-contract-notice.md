# Plaintiff Gave Timely Notice of Breach

Defendant Ostrander Supply Co. argues that Plaintiff Bellweather Foods forfeited its breach-of-contract claim by failing to give timely notice. That argument fails, because Bellweather's notice was timely.

Section 9 of the Supply Agreement requires the non-breaching party to give written notice of any breach within 30 days of the breach. (Supply Agreement, Ex. 1, § 9.)

Ostrander's breach occurred on January 10, when it failed to deliver the first quarterly shipment of packaging materials. (Bellweather Decl. ¶ 6.) On February 7, 28 days later, Bellweather's procurement director emailed Ostrander's operations manager, identifying the missed shipment and stating that Bellweather considered it a breach of the Supply Agreement. (Email of Feb. 7, Ex. 4.)

Bellweather therefore gave notice within the 30-day period required by Section 9. Its notice was timely, and Ostrander's forfeiture defense fails.
